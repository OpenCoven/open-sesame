# OpenCoven identity assets

The crown/lotus mark is the canonical monochrome asset from `OpenCoven/brand`, pinned to commit `798e2f5f2f1a69f5d156ccc0a9aafc4f7da55fc8`.

Source: https://github.com/OpenCoven/brand/blob/798e2f5f2f1a69f5d156ccc0a9aafc4f7da55fc8/web/assets/mark.svg

The SVG path and transform are preserved. The source bytes are vendored as `mark.svg`; the theme script embeds them into the standalone HTML sprite. SHA-256: `3cc1d06c3f957221760984937715f6cde38ac9f69cfa1eb18041c4cbd935c078`.

Brand assets are © OpenCoven. This is an OpenCoven product prototype; redistribution does not transfer rights to the mark. The current `web/profile.json` takes precedence over the older DESIGN.md logo guidance.

The small doorway illustration and familiar initial seals are proposed product UI motifs, not alternate brand marks or canonical familiar portraits. Their appearance conveys no authenticated identity, presence, connection, or permission. The v0.2 serif typography and charcoal/lilac mobile study are retained at Val's direction; this is not a new canonical brand profile. The violet detail uses the existing brand anchor `#8E3DFF`; focus outlines use `#C9A7FF`.
